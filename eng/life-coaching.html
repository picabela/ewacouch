Life Coaching

Life Coaching is for you, if you:

  • Are thinking about a change in your life but don’t know where to start
  • Are at a crossroads thinking what to choose
  • Want to overcome internal blocks and limitations
  • Would like to spread your wings, but feel that as if something is holding you back
  • Want to overcome fear, lack of self-confidence, and conquer self-criticism
  • Want to recognize and reach your full potential
  • Seek new solutions and motivation for action
  • Want to live in harmony with yourself

With Life Coaching, you will:

  • Better identify the goals that suit you
  • Gain more self-confidence
  • Use your skills in a conscious manner
  • Make smarter and better thought-out decisions
  • Overcome internal blocks and limitations
  • Avoid mental “traps”
  • Experience a greater sense of freedom
  • Feel that you can steer the course of your own life

My method:

In my work I apply a solution-focused approach, following the methodology of Erickson College.
During the first meeting I draw a coaching contract together with my client, defining the rules of our co-operation. If the service is sourced by an employer, a trilateral contract is created.

Usually, the process takes from 5 to 10 sessions, which run according to a strictly-defined structure.

The sessions are held every 2-3 weeks.

The time between the sessions is used for implementing the worked-out solutions.

During the sessions I focus on supporting my clients in defining a clear vision of their goals and working out actions to achieve these goals.

As a coach, I abide by the International Coach Federation code of ethics.

My services are addressed to private individuals who wish to develop and change, seek a better quality of life, as well as organizations that intend to advance and support their employees during organizational change – leaders, managers, people taking on challenges, employees changing their carrier paths, or those who are preparing ready for retirement.
