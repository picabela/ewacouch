Career Advisory

Career advisory is for you if you:

  • Are looking for a job
  • Want to change your job/qualifications
  • Need support in building your job search strategy
  • Want to review your competencies
  • Want to learn how to write a professional CV and cover letter
  • Would like to prepare for a job interview
  • Are struggling with mental limitations and stress that keep you from achieving your goals

With Career Advisory, you will:

  • Draw a map of your professional competencies and aptitudes
  • Define your strengths and areas of competitive advantage
  • Set your professional goals and/or target employers
  • Create a professional CV
  • Fully prepare for a job interview
  • Overcome internal blocks and limitations


Please feel free to send me an email or call me in order to make an appointment or ask any questions.
